About DocuSwift Scan

Hello and welcome! We are a small team of developers and students who got tired of using online document scanners that were slow, filled with ads, and worst of all, asked us to upload our personal documents to unknown servers.

Why We Built This Tool

It all started when one of us needed to quickly extract text from a few pages of an old textbook. Every free tool online had a catch: either you could only scan 2 pages a day, or you had to create an account, or the text came out completely garbled.

That's when we decided to build something better. We wanted a tool that just works. No sign-ups, no hidden fees, and absolutely no uploading of your private files. We spent a few weeks coding and testing, and DocuSwift Scan was born.

Our Promise to You

When you use our tool, your images and documents never leave your device. Everything happens right inside your browser using modern JavaScript technology. We don't have a database where we store your files, because we simply don't need one.

Our Core Values

  • ✅ Privacy First: Your documents stay on your device, always.
  • ✅ 100% Free: We believe basic tools should not cost money.
  • ✅ Simplicity: No complex menus, just a fast and clean interface.
  • ✅ Accessibility: Works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and computers.

Who Is This For?

Whether you are a student trying to digitize your notes, a freelancer managing invoices, or someone who just needs to quickly copy text from an image, this tool is for you. We designed it to be fast, reliable, and easy to use for everyone.

We are constantly working to improve the tool. If you have any suggestions, feedback, or just want to say hi, please visit our Contact Us page. We would love to hear from you.

Thank you for choosing DocuSwift Scan. We are glad to have you here.
