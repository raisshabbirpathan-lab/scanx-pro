About DocuSwift Scan

Hi, I'm Raisshabbir Pathan. I built DocuSwift Scan because I was tired of using online document scanners that were slow, filled with annoying ads, and worst of all, asked me to upload my personal documents to unknown servers.

It happened to me last year when I needed to quickly extract text from a few pages of an old textbook. Every free tool online had a catch: either you could only scan 2 pages a day, or you had to create an account, or the text came out completely garbled. I knew there had to be a better way.

Why I Built This Tool

I decided to build a tool that just works. No sign-ups, no hidden fees, and absolutely no uploading of your private files. I spent a few weeks coding and testing, and DocuSwift Scan was born.

The goal was simple: create a fast, browser-based scanner that respects your privacy. Since everything runs locally in your browser, your files never leave your device. You don't have to trust me with your data because I never see it.

My Promise to You

When you use our tool, your images and documents stay on your device. Everything happens right inside your browser using modern JavaScript technology. We don't have a database where we store your files, because we simply don't need one.

Our Core Values

  • ✅ Privacy First: Your documents stay on your device, always.
  • ✅ 100% Free: We believe basic tools should not cost money.
  • ✅ Simplicity: No complex menus, just a fast and clean interface.
  • ✅ Accessibility: Works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and computers.

Who Is This For?

Whether you are a student trying to digitize your notes, a freelancer managing invoices, or someone who just needs to quickly copy text from an image, this tool is for you. We designed it to be fast, reliable, and easy to use for everyone.

Over the last few months, I have also written several detailed guides in our Blog section to help you get the most out of your document scanning. I am constantly working to improve the tool and add more helpful content.
