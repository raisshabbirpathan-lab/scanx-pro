Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is DocuSwift Scan really free?

Yes, DocuSwift Scan is 100% free to use. There are no hidden charges, premium subscriptions, or sign-up requirements. You can scan as many documents as you want.

Are my documents safe and private?

Absolutely. Your privacy is our top priority. All processing happens entirely inside your browser. Your images and extracted text are never uploaded to our servers.

Which languages are supported for OCR?

Currently, we support English, Hindi, and a combination of both (English + Hindi). We are working on adding more languages in the future.

Can I use this tool on my mobile phone?

Yes! DocuSwift Scan is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload any common image format, including JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, and BMP. For best results, use high-resolution, well-lit images.
