🔒 Privacy

DocuSwift Scan is designed to process your document image inside your browser. Your uploaded image and extracted OCR text are not intentionally sent to a DocuSwift server because this website does not use a document-upload backend.

For OCR, the browser loads the Tesseract OCR library and required language data from its CDN. No analytics or advertising tracking code is included in this page.
