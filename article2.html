Tips for Better Document Scanning A good scan starts with a good image. Before scanning any document, make sure the paper is placed on a flat surface. Use proper lighting to avoid shadows. Natural daylight often provides the best results. If the document is folded or damaged, try to flatten it before taking a picture. Keep the camera steady and make sure all corners of the document are visible. Blurry images can reduce the quality of the final scan. For important documents such as certificates, ID cards, and official forms, always review the scanned version before saving or sharing it. Following these simple steps can significantly improve document quality and readability.
