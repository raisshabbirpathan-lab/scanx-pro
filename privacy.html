Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At DocuSwift Scan, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you visit our website and use our document scanning tool.

By using our website, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our tool is built with a "privacy-first" approach. When you upload an image to be scanned:

  • Your files are never uploaded to our servers. All image processing and text extraction (OCR) happens locally inside your web browser.
  • We do not store, save, or share your documents, images, or extracted text.
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the basic features of our tool.

2. Log Data and Analytics

Like most websites, we may collect basic, non-personal information that your browser sends whenever you visit our site. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This data is used purely to understand how our website is being used and to improve the user experience.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

DocuSwift Scan may use "cookies" to store information about your preferences and to record user-specific information on visits. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect how you interact with our site.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising Partners

We may use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other websites to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

6. Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.

7. Consent and Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us through our Contact Us page.
